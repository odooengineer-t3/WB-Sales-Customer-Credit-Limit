Sales Customer Credit Limit

This app helps you control customer credit limits directly from Sales Orders in Odoo. It automatically checks available credit, prevents over-credit sales, and introduces a secure approval workflow when limits are exceeded. Managers can review and approve credit requests using a dedicated wizard, ensuring better financial control while keeping the sales process smooth and efficient.

Features

Define customer-wise credit limits directly from the Customer form.
Automatically validates Sales Orders against available customer credit.
Triggers a credit approval request when the limit is exceeded.
Dedicated approval wizard shows remaining credit and order value clearly.
Restricts credit approval access using role-based security groups.
Real-time calculation of remaining credit based on unpaid invoices.
Enable or disable credit limit enforcement from system settings.
Works seamlessly with existing Sales and Accounting workflows.

1. What does this app do?

This app allows you to set and manage credit limits for customers, automatically checks sales orders against available credit, and provides an approval workflow for orders exceeding the limit.

2. Who can approve credit limit requests?

Only users assigned to the designated approval group can approve or reject credit limit requests, ensuring secure and controlled access.

3. Does this app integrate with standard Odoo Sales and Accounting workflows?

Absolutely. It works seamlessly with existing Sales, Customers, and Accounting modules without modifying the standard workflow.
